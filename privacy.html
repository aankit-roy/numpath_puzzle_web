Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: SEPTEMBER 14, 2026 • NUMPATH PUZZLE

1. Introduction

Welcome to Numpath Puzzle (Package ID: com.aankit.NumpathPuzzle.numpath_puzzle). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Numpath Puzzle mobile application and official companion website.

2. Information We Collect

Numpath Puzzle is built with a strict privacy-first architecture:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect, request, or store your name, email address, physical location, phone number, or contacts.
  • Local Device Storage: Your game progression (unlocked levels across Worlds 1–6, completed stars, daily streak calendar, and chosen visual theme) is stored entirely on your device via secure local storage.
  • Anonymous Gameplay Metrics: We do not operate custom user-tracking servers. Standard anonymous crash reports may be processed via Google Play Services solely to ensure app stability.

3. Offline Play & Data Security

Numpath Puzzle is fully functional offline. The core campaign levels and daily challenge generation do not require an active internet connection or account login. Because your game data remains on your physical hardware, you maintain complete ownership and control over your progress.

4. Third-Party Services

The app interacts with standard Google Play platform services for app distribution, updates, and licensing:

  • Google Play Services: Subject to Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Children’s Privacy

Numpath Puzzle is designed for logic puzzle enthusiasts of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The app is fully compliant with COPPA and Google Play Families guidelines.

6. Changes to This Policy

Any revisions to this privacy policy will be posted directly to this web page with an updated revision date.

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer via Google Play developer support or at: privacy@numpathpuzzle.com.
