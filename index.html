Numpath Puzzle strips away the noise of modern mobile games. No countdown timers pressuring you, no pay-to-win boosters, no intrusive interruptions. Just pure spatial reasoning and the profound satisfaction of completing an unbroken circuit.

✦ CORE RULES

Master the 5 Golden Rules

Simple enough to learn in 10 seconds, deep enough to test your mind across 300 evolving topological labyrinths.

01 START & ORDER

Sequential Checkpoint Order

Your journey always begins at number 1. Trace an unbroken path connecting checkpoints consecutively: 1 → 2 → 3 → 4... until you reach the final number.

02 100% COMPLETION

100% Board Coverage

Reaching the final number is not enough. Every single tile on the grid must be traversed by your line. Leaving even one blank square prevents the stage clear.

03 DIRECTION

Orthogonal Movement Only

Move strictly up, down, left, and right. No diagonal shortcuts allowed. Every step transitions cleanly across tile edges into direct adjacent neighbors.

04 INTEGRITY

No Self-Crossing or Overlaps

Once a tile is occupied, it cannot be traversed again. Your path cannot intersect, loop across itself, or share tiles. Each cell is strictly single-use only.

05 STRATEGY

Forward Path Planning

Beware of dead ends and isolated pockets! A direct route between two numbers might cut off access to corner tiles. Think 3 steps ahead before drawing your line.
