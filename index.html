Numpath Puzzle strips away the noise of modern mobile games. No countdown timers pressuring you, no pay-to-win boosters, no intrusive interruptions. Just pure spatial reasoning and the profound satisfaction of completing an unbroken circuit.

// GAMEPLAY MECHANICS

The 3 Golden Rules

Simple enough to learn in 10 seconds, deep enough to test your mind across 300 evolving labyrinths.

RULE 01

Start at 1, Connect Sequentially

Your journey always begins at number 1. Trace the path and connect numbered checkpoints consecutively: 1 → 2 → 3 → 4... until you reach the final number.

RULE 02

Cover 100% of Every Cell

Reaching the final number is not enough. Every single tile on the grid must be traversed by your line. Leaving even one empty square prevents the stage clear.

RULE 03

Orthogonal Only, No Crossing

Move strictly up, down, left, and right. No diagonal shortcuts. You cannot cross over an already occupied line or visit any tile more than once.
